# Adidas-S-Analytics
# Project Overview
The findings in this project analyzes the Sales performance of Adidas across the United States. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides insights into revenue, profitability, product performance and regional sales trends, enabling data-driven business decisions through dynamic filtering by region and time. 
# Business Problem
To best understand performance and the business, most retail organizations delve into understanding sales performance across products, regions, and retailers to identify growth opportunities and improve profitability. This dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:
Which regions generate the highest sales?
Which products contribute the most revenue?
Which retailers drive the strongest sales performance?
How do sales trends change over time?
# Dataset 
The analysis is based on the Adidas USA sales dataset, containing transactional sales data across multiple states, regions, products, retailers and and time periods.
Tools used; Power BI, Power Query, Microsoft excel ,DAX
Dashboard KPIs
Total sales, Total profit, Total unit sold, Average price per Unit, Average operating Margin, Total sales by month, Total sales by state, Total sales by region, Total sales by product, Total sales by retailer.
Some of the interactive features I used for easy navigation is the regional and time slicer. These filters allow user to explore sales performance across different geographical regions and time periods.
# Key insights
Identified the highest performing sales regions across the United States.
Compared retailer performance to determine the strongest revenue contributor.
Analyzed product-level sales trend to identify top-selling category.
Evaluated monthly sales trends to uncover seasonal patterns.
Assessed profitability using operating margin and total profit metrics to support strategic decision making.
